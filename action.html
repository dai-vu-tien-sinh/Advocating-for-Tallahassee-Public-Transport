Call to Action

The time to modernize StarMetro is Now

Modernizing StarMetro is not only for the residents of Tallahassee, it is proven that it will directly improve the lives of Tallahassee residents. Based on our quantitative data, our proposal uses quantitative evidence such as the Public Transport Accessibility Index for Residents (PTAIR) to prove that decentralizing transit hubs decrease destination travel times and eliminates suburban transit deserts. Financially, shifting just 15% of daily car trips to public transit prevents a lot of associated costs, such as pavement degradation, yiledin millions of dollars in cumulative road maintenance savings over ten years that can directly offset StarMetro expansion costs. Beyond the numbers, this overhaul addresses travel needs of a lot of needy residents. University students and working-class families in areas like Frenchtown and Southside, while transit loss accelerates cognitive decline and independence among senior citizens. The peer-reviewed research on decentralized transit and last-mile bicycle integration provides this proposal with a grounded, evidence-based perspective. This proposal provides the Tallahassee City Commission and StarMetro planners with a proven roadmap to approve a pilot decentralization program, adopt PTAIR standards, and fund last-mile infrastructure for a more well-connected city.
